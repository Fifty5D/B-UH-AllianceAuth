HOW IT WORKS

Audit trail

  1. 1
    ObserveCorporation observer ledgers identify every character and ore unit at owned refineries.
  2. 2
    ValueEditable compression mappings convert ore to compressed equivalents and fill current Jita buy orders.
  3. 3
    BillMining is grouped by Auth account by default and locked to the extraction’s effective tax rate.
  4. 4
    ReviewWallet and pure-ISK contract payments remain pending until a director allocates or classifies them.

CCP history limitESI exposes roughly 30 days for observer ledgers, wallet journals, and contracts. Imported rows are kept permanently here.
